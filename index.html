Trade suggestions

Realistic starting points, not final offers — matched so both sides plausibly gain something. You'll still need to actually negotiate these with the other manager.
Note: Sleeper doesn't expose an actual "trade block" list through its public data (checked directly — the field it would live in is empty across every team in your league, and the League Chat feed loads over a connection this app can't read), so these are generated from roster surplus/need analysis across the whole league, not from anyone's declared willingness to trade a specific player.
